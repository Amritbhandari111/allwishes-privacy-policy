Privacy Policy — Birthday Invitation Maker

Last updated: May 18, 2026 · Country: Nepal

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Birthday Invitation Maker collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the app. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Definitions

Application: Birthday Invitation Maker
Company: The developer of Birthday Invitation Maker
Device: Any smartphone or tablet that can access the Service
Service: The Application
You: The individual using the Service

Age Restriction

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Collection & Usage

The app is designed to work mainly on your device. Some features may use online services for ads, crash reporting, cloud templates and optional AI text generation.

User-Generated Content

  • Users may type invitation text, birthday wishes, names, dates, venue details, RSVP details and reminder notes.
  • Users may select photos or images from their device gallery for editing.
  • Edited images and editable designs may be stored locally on the device so users can save, share or edit them again.
  • The app does not sell your photos, designs, reminders or greeting content.

AI Text Features

  • The app may offer optional AI text features to rewrite invitation wording, generate birthday wishes or improve greeting messages.
  • If you use AI features, the text you enter and relevant template text may be sent to an AI service provider to generate a response.
  • You should not enter sensitive personal information into AI prompts.
  • AI features are optional and are used only when you choose to use them.

Template Loading & Caching

  • Some templates, backgrounds, thumbnails, fonts or design elements may be hosted on cloud storage/CDN services, including Cloudflare R2.
  • The app may download a manifest JSON file to show available cloud templates and assets.
  • Downloaded templates and assets may be cached on the device to improve speed and reduce data usage.
  • Cloud template downloads contain design files and images; they do not require users to create an account.

Crash Reporting

The app may use Firebase Crashlytics or similar Google/Firebase services to collect crash reports, including device information, app version, operating system version and stack traces. This helps us identify bugs, improve performance and reduce crashes.

Advertising Data

The app may use Google AdMob to show ads, including app open ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads and banner ads. AdMob may collect or use advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app activity and similar technical data to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

The app itself does not require users to provide:

  • Account registration
  • Email address or phone number
  • GPS location
  • Contacts

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Used to display ads and measure ad performance. Google may process advertising identifiers, device data and technical information according to Google’s own policies.

Firebase Crashlytics / Google Services

Used to collect crash reports and diagnostic information so we can improve app stability and performance.

Cloudflare R2 or CDN Services

Used to host and deliver templates, thumbnails, backgrounds, fonts and design assets. Basic network information such as IP address and request data may be processed by the hosting provider when assets are downloaded.

AI Service Provider

Used only when you choose optional AI text features. Text submitted for AI generation may be processed by the AI service provider to return invitation text, birthday wishes or greeting messages.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared only when needed to provide app features, such as ads, crash reporting, cloud template delivery or optional AI text generation, or when required by law. If you choose to share, print or export a design, the action is handled by your device and the third-party app or service you select.

Offline Functionality

Many editing features can work offline after local or cached assets are available. Features that require internet, such as ads, cloud template downloads or optional AI text generation, may not work without a network connection.

Data Retention

Saved designs, editable drafts and reminders may remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. Cached cloud assets may be stored temporarily to improve performance. Crash reports, advertising data and AI request data may be retained by third-party service providers according to their own policies.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app data and reduce unauthorised access. User designs and reminders are primarily stored locally on the device. Online resources are generally accessed through secure HTTPS connections. However, no app, device or internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

  • You can delete saved designs and reminders inside the app where available.
  • You can disable notifications from your device settings.
  • You can limit ad personalisation through Google or device settings.
  • You can avoid using AI features if you do not want prompt text sent to an AI service provider.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

Changes to This Policy
